Hey! I'm Sammie Crisman🙏

I'm a frontend software engineer with a strong desire to build responsive applications that are performant on the web, these being inclusive and user friendly. My aim is to build things so I can help the online world be a better place. I like to design stuff with the elderly in mind in particular, our world and AI are rapidly changing, growing, and expanding, my aim is to help everyone have access even people with no technical experience from all walks of life. Not everyone knows how to protect themselves from online threats. Everyday, unknowingly people put themselves and their family at risk without even realizing it, all because they weren't educated in proper online hygiene. There is a lot that goes into protecting your safety, security and identity when using technology and it varies depending on what devices are being used. I'd like to help protect innocent people from the criminals of the online world, the police are not always equipped to handle these matters. We the people must take our country back. Honest people deserve honest protection. We are blessed to be alive and everyday we should be using the gifts God gave us to better the world and honor him.
